## About Me

I'm a full-stack software engineer based in Auckland, New Zealand, with over 10 years of experience across desktop, mobile, web, real-time media, cloud, and mission-critical systems.

My work has taken me through lead and senior engineering roles in Singapore and Myanmar, where I have built production software with C#, .NET, TypeScript, React, React Native, real-time streaming, geospatial mapping, Azure infrastructure, and CI/CD delivery.

I enjoy turning complex operational requirements into software that is clear to use, reliable in production, and maintainable for the teams that inherit it. I care about architecture, implementation quality, testing, mentoring, and the practical details that make delivery work outside the happy path.

I am open to remote or flexible part-time software engineering opportunities where I can contribute full-stack depth, product judgment, and technical leadership.
